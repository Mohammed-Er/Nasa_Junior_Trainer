Situation Overview: The Silence from Earth

You are the Commander of ARES-1, an experimental four-astronaut research outpost situated in the Martian highlands. At 04:12 UTC, telemetry signals from Earth's orbital relay network suddenly went dead.

An immediate rescue from Earth is impossible—Mars and Earth are tens of millions of kilometers apart. Your core directive is simple yet vital: keep your crew and habitat functional for 30 Martian days (sols), investigate the cause of the blackout, and make the critical decisions necessary to re-establish the link with Earth.
